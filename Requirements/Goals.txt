Our primary goal is to build a pro-bono application for food pantries that streamlines intake, tracking, and distribution of food items, 
while providing robust reporting and operational tools to support efficient pantry management.

Our design stresses simplicity of use and accessibility, ensuring that volunteers and staff of all technical skill levels can efficiently manage pantry operations.

Our secondary goal is to learn by working through the tough problems, doing our research, and break into the developer industry 
by gaining hands-on experience, real-world problem-solving, and building a portfolio of meaningful projects, starting with this one 
built for a noble cause.
