Intake

Donor tracking: who gave what, with a simple donor list
Donation receipts for tax purposes
Batch intake for big deliveries, such as scanning one item and entering a quantity
Photo capture for items with no barcode
Weight-based entry for produce and bulk goods

Tracking

Categories and tags (shelf-stable, refrigerated, frozen, allergen-friendly, halal, kosher, baby items)
Storage locations (shelf, freezer, back room)
Cycle counts: a quick "check this shelf" mode to fix drift between the app and reality
Waste logging: what was discarded and why
Item history: the full life of any item, from intake to checkout

Distribution

Client check-in and visit tracking (with privacy limits)
Household size limits or "points" systems for client-choice pantries
Pre-built boxes or bags for quick pickup
Online ordering and pickup scheduling (phase two)
Dietary needs matching

Reporting

Pounds distributed, households served, and top-needed items
Reports for funders and grant applications, which pantries often need to produce
Reports in the formats food bank networks require, such as for regional food banks

Team and operations

Roles and permissions (volunteer, manager, admin)
Volunteer shift tracking
Audit log of who did what
Multi-location support for pantries with more than one site

Technical

Offline mode with sync
CSV import and export, since many pantries start in spreadsheets
Wishlist or "needed items" list that can be shared with donors

Priorities

MVP: scanning, intake, inventory list, checkout, expiration, low stock
Next: categories, locations, waste log, CSV import and export, reports
Later: client features, ordering, donor management, multi-location
